# Paso a Paso · Spanish Lab

A small offline web app for learning beginner Spanish on an iPad, about 10 minutes a day.

**Open it:** https://rmaul1-dev.github.io/spanish-lab/
On an iPad, open the link in Safari and tap Share → **Add to Home Screen**.

- Latin American Spanish, taught with pictures instead of translations: words → phrases → sentences → short conversations
- Listening, reading, speaking, spelling (with á é í ó ú ñ ¿ ¡ keys) and sentence building in every lesson
- Grammar is discovered from color-coded patterns, then summed up on a one-line pattern card
- Spaced review of missed words, two player profiles, a gentle 10-minute timer, stars and streaks
- A grown-up view with progress, minutes per day and trouble words
- No accounts, ads or tracking. Progress stays on the device (localStorage). The only network use is the browser's own speech recognition, if you choose to use the microphone.

All lessons, pictures and wording are original. Not affiliated with any language-learning company.
